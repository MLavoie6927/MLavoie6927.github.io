## Key verified facts and caveats

1. States deliver their own tests. The federal knowledge-test minimum is **80% on each component**, and general knowledge tests must contain at least 30 items; do not assume the 95-question app format is an official state exam structure.
2. The Arkansas base manual PDF is hosted in an August 21, 2025 directory path but identifies its content as a **July 2017 manual**; modernized skills guidance exists in a separate **September 2022 supplement**. Always check for a newer official state-issued manual or supplemental directions before studying or testing.
3. For Arkansas modernized vehicle inspection, the cited supplement states **applied leak: 3 psi/min for a single vehicle or 4 psi/min for a combination**; **low-air warning before 55 psi or manufacturer threshold**, **typical spring-brake control pop-out 20–45 psi**, and **governor cutoff approximately 120–140 psi**; physical vehicle specifications govern.
4. Federal property-carrier HOS rules have exceptions and should not be used without understanding qualifications.
5. The 80,000 / 20,000 / 34,000 lb figures concern typical federal **Interstate** limits and remain subject to bridge, route and equipment requirements.
6. ELDT for Class A/B and S/P skills tests generally precedes the skills examination; first-time H theory completion is required before H knowledge examination, subject to applicable exemptions.
7. This bank contains 300 **original explanatory practice questions**. None are asserted to be official licensing test items. No licensed AAMVA textbook content or question bank is reproduced.
