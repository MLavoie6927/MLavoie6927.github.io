# Regulatory and source notes — reviewed 2026-11-17

This document deliberately distinguishes federal requirements, specific state/city rules, and operational good practice.

## Highway navigation
- FMCSA advises truck-appropriate GPS and **always obey** traffic signs and advisories: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/faq/what-tips-fmcsa-providing-safe-use-gps-navigation-systems
- 49 CFR § 392.2 relates to compliance with applicable motor-vehicle operating laws: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.2
- FHWA states that **states may set their own height restrictions**; there is no federal nationwide height number: https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/freight/sw/faqs/qa.cfm
- FHWA publishes federal size/weight background; do not confuse maximum federal interstate gross weights with actual allowable load on a route: https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/FREIGHT/publications/size_regs_final_rpt/

## Cargo / equipment / driver hours
- 49 CFR § 392.9 inspection of cargo/securement; exceptions and inspection timing must be read in the current actual text: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.9
- Cargo rules: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/cargo-securement/cargo-securement-rules
- Hours-of-service summary: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/hours-service/summary-hours-service-regulations
- Hazardous conditions: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.14
- Railroad crossings: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.10 and https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.11
- Hazmat routing: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-397

## Editorial rules
- The original supplied baseline included 98 scenarios, not 90 (sum of its original 8 categories). This expanded catalog includes 222.
- Real-world examples are **specific documented restrictions**, not evidence that a particular GPS currently misroutes a driver there.
- Do not assert that a site/country/state policy is a federal DOT regulation.
- Do not assert an application validates a road, bridge, 53-ft trailer route or overweight configuration unless using an actual, reviewed routing authority feed.
- Driver observations must not silently convert to legal restrictions or automatically reroute an entire fleet.
- Authorities may update content; refresh all research before public compliance claims.
