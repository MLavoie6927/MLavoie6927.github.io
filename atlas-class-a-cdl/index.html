Stop / verify / escalate

Low bridge or clearance conflict

Do not approach the obstruction. Stop in a safe location, verify actual height and posted data, call dispatch and obtain a lawful alternate route.

Unsafe dock or gate

Do not force a backing maneuver. Coordinate with authorized facility personnel, request appropriate traffic management or alternate unloading.

Overweight axle

Stop dispatch progression, obtain an accurate scale ticket, request rework or tandem adjustment when permitted and reweigh.

Railroad crossing obstruction

Keep the entire vehicle out until adequate space exists. If trapped on tracks, get clear and notify railroad emergency control and 911.

Dispatch conflicts with signs

Follow applicable law and traffic controls. Document the conflict and request corrected instructions without risking a citation or crash.
