How to interpret the data

Published reference

Detroit DD15 43 L oil-and-filter service fill for applicable configurations; federal gross and axle weight rules.

Generic system illustration

Symbols and architecture demonstrate functional relationships, not physical wiring, air-line, plumbing or service routing.

Manufacturer verification

Vehicle VIN, engine and transmission serials, tires, axle tags and OEM publications determine final service/operating parameters.

Repair prices / service intervals

Part costs, labor times and editable service schedules are rough examples, not live quotes or validated OEM service intervals.

Safety

Never use a training simulator to authorize operating an unsafe vehicle, releasing charged spring brakes, pressurizing lines, or conducting repairs.
