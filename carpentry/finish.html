Finish carpentry

Educational reference. Follow approved plans, local building codes, permits, and manufacturer instructions. Use qualified trades for structural, electrical, plumbing, and other regulated work.

Finish carpentry turns a structurally complete space into a precise, durable interior. The best results begin with measured openings, stable materials, and a consistent reveal plan.

Work areas

  • Doors: check opening, plumb, level, swing, clearance, and hardware before casing.
  • Base and casing: plan reveals, joint locations, transitions, and fastening into suitable backing.
  • Cabinetry and built-ins: locate wall structure, level support, service clearances, and manufacturer attachment requirements.
  • Stairs and rails: use approved dimensions and code compliant handrails and guards.

Quality approach

  1. Acclimate materials as the manufacturer directs.
  2. Measure actual site conditions; verify corners and walls before production cuts.
  3. Dry fit visible joints and protect adjacent finishes.
  4. Fasten, fill, sand, and finish to the specified system.
  5. Check operation, alignment, sharp edges, and final cleanup.

Reference: OSHA woodworking safety. For product specific installation, follow the door, trim, cabinet, adhesive, and finish manufacturer instructions.
