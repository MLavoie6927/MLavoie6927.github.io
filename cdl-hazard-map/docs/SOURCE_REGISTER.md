## Verification and attribution policy

- This document cites **official sources for the underlying statements**. Re-check documents, signs, permits and conditions before real-world use.
- **Official** means a public government/road-authority publication supports the text; it is not certification of the map coordinates or a live closure status.
- **Illustrative** markers are regional concerns, not allegedly verified hazards, crash counts, reported bridge strikes or currently restricted roads. No official-source claim is attached to these entries.
- Heat shading highlights concentration of curated entries and is not a statistical hazard rate or accident frequency.
- The dashed corridor overlays are schematic hub connections, **not actual road centerlines, legal truck network routes, or proposed driving paths**.
- State boundaries originate from a simplified reference county boundary collection bundled with the local Basemap package, reprojected into offline SVG state shapes. Geometries are for display and do not establish jurisdictional precision for road restrictions.
- All source links were researched as of the reference date and may change or become outdated.

## General FMCSA GPS guidance

FMCSA states that commercial operators should select truck/bus-intended navigation and always obey posted road signs and advisories, even when GPS differs: [FMCSA GPS tips](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/faq/what-tips-fmcsa-providing-safe-use-gps-navigation-systems).
