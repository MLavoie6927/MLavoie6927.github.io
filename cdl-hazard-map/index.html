IMPORTANT MAP LIMITATION

All orange highlighted road traces and markers are schematic regional references, not legal route geometry or verified GPS guidance. These entries summarize government-published restrictions and operating conditions, but do not prove that a particular GPS product will send a truck there. Temporary conditions change. Vehicle configuration, route segments, signed exceptions, hazmat classifications and jurisdictional rules require authoritative checks before driving. Neither the map nor its profile comparison can certify a road as safe or legal.
