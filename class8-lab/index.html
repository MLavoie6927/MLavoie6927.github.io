CLASS A CLP FOCUS

Know exactly what this permit guide covers.

FMCSA sets minimum federal standards, but states administer Commercial Learner's Permits and develop their own tests. The current manual for the licensing state controls the exact subjects, procedures, and wording.

35 questions · included

General knowledge

Driving safety, inspections, cargo, stopping distance, visibility, emergencies, vehicle control, ratings, and operating decisions.

20 questions · included

Air brakes

Air pressure, compressors, reservoirs, warning devices, spring brakes, leakage tests, dryers, gauges, and defect response.

15 questions · included

Combination vehicles

Tractor-trailer operation, fifth wheels, kingpins, air lines, coupling, uncoupling, off-tracking, and rollover prevention.

Separate tests · verify locally

Additional endorsements

Tank vehicles, hazardous materials, passenger vehicles, school buses, and doubles or triples can require additional knowledge, training, or testing. They are not represented by this 70-question set.
