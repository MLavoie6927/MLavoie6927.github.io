CLASS A CDL · OPERATING SAFETY · CONSERVATIVE DECISIONS

Turn CDL knowledge into safer operating decisions.

The ATLAS Class A CDL Safety Studio adds a searchable operating-scenario library, decision drills, a predeparture field guide, conservative vehicle-versus-restriction comparisons, dispatch messaging, and local-only hazard documentation. It never grants route approval or replaces current traffic controls, state law, permits, carrier policy, or qualified professional judgment.

222 Class A operating scenarios 14 categories · 24 decision drills · 28 readiness checks
01

Investigate scenarios

Search low-clearance, weight, cargo, coupling, backing, dock, weather, hours-of-service, hazmat, specialty-equipment, and communication problems by category and severity.

02

Stop, verify, escalate

Compare known vehicle dimensions with entered restrictions. Missing information stays VERIFY; a direct conflict becomes STOP; no output is presented as route authorization.

03

Document responsibly

Build a dispatcher message and keep browser-local hazard notes separated as unverified observations, observed signs, or source-reviewed assertions.

04

Practice decisions

Work 24 original drills with explanations, complete a 28-item readiness checklist, and follow links to current federal, state, and local authority pages.

Educational system—not truck GPS.

The studio has no live map, telemetry, legal-route database, permit checker, bridge analysis, or agency verification. Do not enter personal, confidential-shipment, or sensitive employer information. Posted signs, current authoritative sources, the actual vehicle and load, and applicable operating rules control real-world decisions.

CLASS A CDL · U.S. GEOGRAPHY · GPS CONFLICT AWARENESS

See the restriction before the road becomes a trap.

The ATLAS U.S. Class A CDL GPS Trap & Hazard Atlas turns route-planning risk into an explorable visual system. It maps all 50 states plus Washington, D.C.; separates 30 government-sourced location records from 50 illustrative planning zones; and adds 30 documented named-road profiles across 15 states for low clearances, no-truck parkways, tight curves, length and weight conflicts, tunnel or hazmat rules, and weather restrictions.

30 Named road profiles 15 states · 51 jurisdictions · 80 mapped locations
01

Explore U.S. risk

Pan and zoom the offline map, select a state, filter categories and status, expand clusters, and compare documented locations without an API key or live tracking.

02

Investigate road conflicts

Open the orange GPS-trap layer and search named roads by state, category, or issue. Each profile links the restriction or operational guidance to its cited authority.

03

Compare the actual rig

Enter measured height, length, gross and registered weight, and KPRA values. The tool reports only possible conflicts or unknowns—it never certifies access.

04

Separate evidence types

Source-backed entries, illustrative planning zones, schematic road traces, and curated-density heat shading remain visibly distinct so a reader can judge what each layer proves.

Visual atlas—not truck navigation.

The map is offline and historical, not a current traffic feed, dispatch approval, surveyed road network, complete clearance registry, or proof that a specific GPS product routed a driver incorrectly. Schematic lines are not drivable paths. Posted signs, current agency information, permits, carrier policy, and the actual vehicle and load control every real-world routing decision.

CLASS A CLP FOCUS

Know exactly what this permit guide covers.

FMCSA sets minimum federal standards, but states administer Commercial Learner's Permits and develop their own tests. The current manual for the licensing state controls the exact subjects, procedures, and wording.

35 questions · included

General knowledge

Driving safety, inspections, cargo, stopping distance, visibility, emergencies, vehicle control, ratings, and operating decisions.

20 questions · included

Air brakes

Air pressure, compressors, reservoirs, warning devices, spring brakes, leakage tests, dryers, gauges, and defect response.

15 questions · included

Combination vehicles

Tractor-trailer operation, fifth wheels, kingpins, air lines, coupling, uncoupling, off-tracking, and rollover prevention.

Separate tests · verify locally

Class A endorsements

Tank vehicles, hazardous materials, and doubles or triples can require additional knowledge, training, or testing. They are not represented by this 70-question foundation but are available in the Class A Academy.
