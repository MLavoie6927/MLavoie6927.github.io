How U.S. government works

Educational U.S. civics guide. Offices, powers, procedures, and current officeholders vary by location and time. Verify your issue with an official agency and current law.

Federal and national name the same level in the United States. State governments have their own constitutions and institutions. Local governments exercise authority defined by their states. A single service can involve law, funding, delivery, and oversight from different places.
