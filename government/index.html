How U.S. government works

U.S. civics overview. Offices, powers, and procedures vary by state and locality. Check current official sources for your address and issue.

In the United States, federal and national refer to the same level of government. State governments have their own constitutions and institutions. Local governments operate under authority defined by their states.

Local government

City, county, town, school, and special district offices and services.

State government

Governors, legislatures, courts, agencies, and local authority.

Federal / national government

Congress, the president and agencies, and federal courts.
