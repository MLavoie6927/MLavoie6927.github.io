LUSOVELA · WINDOWS BROWSER

Privacy Policy

Understand what stays on your device, what connects to outside services, and how you control your browser data.

Published by Marc Lavoie · Effective September 26, 2026 · Applies to LusoVela Windows version 1.4.17

Your LusoVela account is local.

Your browser history, settings, and security records stay on your device. LusoVela does not operate an account-sync service or upload browser analytics. Websites, search providers, domain protection, and protected-media services still receive the information needed to provide their services.

1. Scope and publisher

Marc Lavoie publishes LusoVela Browser. This policy describes the Windows desktop application and this privacy-policy page. Websites and services you visit have their own privacy policies, including Microsoft Store, search providers, banks, and streaming services.

2. Information stored on your device

LusoVela stores browser history, bookmarks, preferences, local account records, security decisions, recovery checkpoints, investigation cases, and security diagnostics on your device. These records support browsing, session recovery, and explanations of security decisions.

The current build has no LusoVela advertising SDK, remote news feed, account-sync service, or LusoVela analytics uploader. Marc Lavoie does not receive your local browser records through an automatic LusoVela reporting service.

Investigation cases can include page addresses with query strings and fragments removed, limited connection information, policy-event summaries, and notes you enter. Case snapshots omit page contents, passwords, full request logs, download records, and screenshots. You control whether to export or share a case; exported files may still contain sensitive notes or addresses.

3. Local username, password, and download protection

Your chosen username and salted password-verification record are protected using Electron safeStorage and Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). These credentials remain on your device. The local login and download lock help control access and authorize downloads; they do not encrypt the entire browser profile or prevent capture by software already controlling your Windows account.

Website downloads are privately staged, checked by available local controls, and require local password authorization before release. Download metadata and security results may be recorded locally.

4. Connections to websites and outside services

  • Websites and searches: websites receive ordinary connection and request information, including your IP address. Search terms go to the search provider you select. Services receive information you submit, such as sign-in details, messages, or documents.
  • Your DNS choice: system DNS is the default and follows your Windows configuration, including your own DNS provider, network, or VPN preferences. LusoVela does not automatically select Quad9. In Privacy & Security you can opt into Quad9 DNS over HTTPS or enter your own HTTPS DNS endpoint. This choice stays on your device. Upgrading from a release without an explicit DNS mode starts in system mode.
  • Optional Quad9 protection: Quad9 can block known malicious domains. When selected, it receives domain queries and your network IP. The DNS feature does not upload URL paths, page contents, documents, cookies, or passwords. See Quad9’s privacy policy.
  • Custom encrypted DNS: your chosen provider receives domain queries and your network IP under its own policy. Custom providers may not block threats. Local ad, tracker, and domain filters remain active in every DNS mode.
  • Protected media: CastLabs/Widevine component initialization and component-update checks may contact external component services. Streaming providers also make their own playback and license requests under their policies.
  • Local filters: this release has no configured LusoVela software-update or signed-filter feed. Built-in filters are evaluated locally.

Quad9 and custom encrypted DNS modes do not fall back to unencrypted DNS when the chosen service is unavailable. Existing streams may reconnect after a change. Local hosts, cached addresses, and proxy-resolved traffic may not undergo a fresh lookup through the selected service. Domain protection is not remote file scanning and cannot guarantee that every site is safe.

5. Local filtering and website permissions

Built-in filters and configured, cryptographically signed domain-filter packs are evaluated locally. Protection Activity stores explanations for blocked requests and tracking cookies. Temporary compatibility allowances are limited to the current site and tab, expire automatically, and are unavailable in Banking and Malware Analysis profiles.

Websites may request camera, microphone, location, notifications, clipboard, or other permissions. LusoVela applies profile and secure-origin rules before prompting. If you grant a permission, the requesting website may receive the corresponding data.

6. Your controls and data retention

You can select system DNS, optional Quad9, or a custom HTTPS DNS provider in Privacy & Security. An optional Português (Portugal) toggle changes supported browser interface labels locally, including menus, settings, login, and the new-tab page. No translation service is contacted. Websites, legal notices, and user-entered content keep their original language.

Browser controls let you clear selected history, cookies, cache, site storage, recovery snapshots, and local security records. Clear-on-exit can automate browser cleanup. Local data remains until removed by browser controls, applicable cleanup settings, or deletion of its stored files.

Clearing browser data does not remove downloaded files saved outside the browser, exported investigation files, external backups, operating-system records, or records retained by websites and service providers. Local login records are separate from routine browsing-history cleanup.

7. Private and disposable sessions

Private windows use isolated in-memory browser storage and are not restored as session history. Disposable profiles use separate containers and clear their container data according to their configured lifetime. These modes do not hide network traffic from websites, employers, internet providers, network operators, or your operating system.

8. Children and security

LusoVela is a general-purpose browser and does not operate a service intended to collect children’s personal information. Websites visited through the browser remain responsible for their own data practices.

No browser provides complete protection against malicious sites, unknown vulnerabilities, compromised devices, or unsafe actions. Keep Windows and browser security releases current. Review exported diagnostics and cases before sharing them.

9. This policy page

This page uses no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts, cookies, or browser storage. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s hosting documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement.

10. Contact, support, and policy changes

For privacy questions or LusoVela support, contact Marc Lavoie through the publisher’s GitHub support channel. Identify LusoVela and its version. GitHub issues are public: do not include passwords, private browsing records, financial information, or sensitive documents. GitHub may require an account to submit a request.

If you contact the publisher, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request. GitHub processes and retains submissions under its own policy. You can ask the publisher to remove information from a support issue they control; third-party records and backups may remain.

Material changes to this policy will be published with an updated effective date and corresponding release information.
