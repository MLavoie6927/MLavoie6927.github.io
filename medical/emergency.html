Emergency response

Educational information, not individual medical advice. For an emergency, call your local emergency number. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about your medicines.

Call your local emergency number now for severe trouble breathing, chest pain, sudden weakness or speech trouble, uncontrolled bleeding, severe allergic reaction, loss of consciousness, or suspected overdose.

While help is coming

  • Follow the dispatcher’s instructions and give your location.
  • Keep the person safe; avoid moving them unless there is immediate danger.
  • Share medicine, allergy, and condition information with responders.
  • Use CPR or an AED as directed by training or emergency dispatch.

For suspected poisoning in the U.S., call Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222. Elsewhere, contact your local poison service. Source: CDC emergency warning signs.
