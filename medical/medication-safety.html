Medicine combinations & safety

Educational information, not individual medical advice. For an emergency, call your local emergency number. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about your medicines.

No combination is universally safe. Safety depends on exact ingredients, dose, timing, age, pregnancy, allergies, health conditions, and other medicines or supplements.

Before taking two products

  1. List prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements.
  2. Compare active ingredients on labels to avoid accidental duplicates.
  3. Read warnings and follow each labeled dose.
  4. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber to check the exact combination.
  5. Keep an updated medication list for appointments.

Do not stop a prescribed medicine because its name resembles another. For suspected overdose or poisoning, contact poison control or emergency services promptly.

Sources: MedlinePlus drug information; OTC medicines; questions for your provider.
