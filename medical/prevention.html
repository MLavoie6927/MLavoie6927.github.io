Common problems & prevention

Educational information, not individual medical advice. For an emergency, call your local emergency number. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about your medicines.

Respiratory illness

Wash hands, keep recommended vaccines current, improve ventilation where practical, and stay home when sick. CDC hand hygiene

Heart and metabolic health

Know your blood pressure, discuss appropriate screening, stay active, and follow your chronic care plan. CDC preventive care

Medication errors

Keep one current medication list and compare ingredients before buying combination products. MedlinePlus OTC guide

Time and cost savers

Prepare questions before visits, keep test results, ask about appropriate generic options, and use recommended preventive care. Savings vary.

Seek assessment for persistent, worsening, or unexplained symptoms.
