Medical word parts

Educational information, not individual medical advice. For an emergency, call your local emergency number. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about your medicines.

Medical words combine Latin and Greek elements. This practical selection is not an exhaustive list of prefixes or medicine names. Word parts cannot establish treatment safety.

Common prefixes

  • hyper-: above normal, as in hypertension
  • hypo-: below normal, as in hypoglycemia
  • tachy-: fast, as in tachycardia
  • brady-: slow, as in bradycardia
  • endo-: inside, as in endoscopy
  • peri-: around, as in pericardium
  • trans-: across, as in transdermal
  • inter-: between, as in intercostal
  • micro-: small
  • macro-: large

Roots and endings

cardi/o heart · derm/o skin · gastr/o stomach · neur/o nerve · pharmac/o drug · -itis inflammation · -algia pain · -ectomy surgical removal

Sources: MedlinePlus word parts; understanding medical words.
