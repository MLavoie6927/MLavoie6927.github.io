01 / Complete role inventory

Nine standard Intune built-in roles

These are the roles cataloged in the source paper. The descriptions are concise research summaries; Microsoft’s permission matrix remains the authority for each action.

RolePrimary workAccess review question
Application ManagerManage and assign managed and mobile apps.Does the task require managed-app wipe or broad app deployment?
Endpoint Privilege ManagerAuthor and assign Endpoint Privilege Management policies and act on elevation requests.Who may approve privileged execution?
Endpoint Privilege ReaderReview EPM policy, reports and elevation requests.Can review stay read-only?
Endpoint Security ManagerManage security and compliance controls.Are the broader policy and remote-action rights warranted?
Help Desk OperatorSupport users and devices, including permitted remote tasks.Does support need powerful actions such as retire or wipe?
Intune Role AdministratorCreate custom roles and delegate Intune role assignments.Who independently reviews new delegation?
Policy and Profile ManagerManage compliance and configuration policies and profiles.Could a broad assignment affect the entire fleet?
Read Only OperatorView device, user, policy and report information.Could its read rights expose sensitive recovery material?
School AdministratorManage assigned education groups and devices.Is the education scope actually required?

Why the short name is not enough: the paper highlights that Help Desk Operator includes destructive remote actions and Read Only Operator can include access to FileVault recovery keys. A custom read role may be safer for an analyst who only needs compliance reports.

02 / Applied design

Which role would I use for this project?

This is the proposed division of work for the fictional 20-device Intune × Sentinel pilot. It deliberately separates Intune policy editing, Entra access policy control, evidence review and approval.

Custom Intune compliance editor

Build the pilot policy

Give a dedicated endpoint engineering group the compliance Read, Create, Update, Assign and report rights needed for the pilot. Limit the assignment to the approved pilot scope group and NS-WIN-PILOT scope tag. Exclude Delete and destructive device actions.

Why custom? Policy and Profile Manager has broader policy authority than this one task needs.

Custom Intune security observer

Review device evidence

Give the SOC compliance Read, View reports and managed-device Read for the pilot. No Update, Assign, Wipe, Retire or recovery-key access.

Why custom? Read Only Operator can include sensitive reads; Endpoint Security Manager is too powerful for observation.

Entra Conditional Access Administrator

Control the sign-in policy

Identity engineering creates the separate pilot CA policy, checks Report-only sign-in results, and owns the exact policy’s enforcement or return to Report-only. This Entra role does not grant the Intune compliance edit.

Gate: require a distinct approval record before turning the pilot policy On.

Intune Role Administrator

Own delegation

A separate delegation owner creates and assigns the custom Intune roles. Check all administrator groups, scope groups and scope tags; test allowed and forbidden actions using representative identities.

Gate: a surprising out-of-scope edit holds the rollout.

Custom MAA approver role

Review protected changes

Use a distinct, directly assigned approver group with the resource-specific Read permission required for supported Multi Admin Approval requests. The requester cannot approve their own change.

Gate: retain the request, reviewer, decision and audit record.

Sentinel analyst access

Investigate the alert

Assign the appropriate Azure/Sentinel reader or responder rights at the intended workspace/resource scope, depending on whether the analyst only reviews or also manages incidents. A Logic App has its own narrowly scoped identity.

Boundary: Sentinel access is separate from Intune RBAC and Entra CA administration.

Roles I would not grant just to make the pilot work: Global Administrator, Entra Intune Administrator, broad Endpoint Security Manager, or Help Desk Operator. Each may confer authority beyond the named job. A support agent needing a specific remote task would get a separately reviewed, tightly scoped role.

03 / Effective access

Four boundaries that must agree

Policy assignmentWho or which device receives the compliance policy.
Role permissionWhich administrative action the operator can take.
Scope groupWhich users and devices that admin may manage.
Scope tagWhich tagged Intune objects that admin can see.

Negative test from the paper

An administrator has Read for compliance policies tagged NS-NORTH and Update under a second assignment tagged NS-SOUTH. Under documented default behavior, same-category permissions across assignments can merge more broadly than expected. Attempt a harmless North test edit with a disposable object. If it succeeds, stop and repair the overlapping membership.

Evidence before approval

  1. Export the exact admin and pilot group IDs.
  2. Review the role permission matrix and every assignment.
  3. Compare scope groups, tags and effective permission reports.
  4. Perform one allowed and one forbidden test.
  5. Record the independent approver’s decision.

An Intune assignment filter narrows policy targeting. It does not constrain a Conditional Access policy, replace an Intune RBAC scope group, or make a scope tag a deployment target.

04 / Adjacent roles

Entra and Windows 365 are different layers

The source paper inventories eleven Entra roles shown in Microsoft’s Intune access table: Global Administrator, Intune Administrator, Conditional Access Administrator, Security Administrator, Security Operator, Security Reader, Compliance Administrator, Compliance Data Administrator, Global Reader, Helpdesk Administrator and Reports Reader.

Entra Intune Administrator

Broad Intune authority from Entra is distinct from Intune Role Administrator. Do not assume Intune scope tags limit this global role.

Conditional Access Administrator

Controls the sign-in decision layer. It is assigned for the pilot CA policy, not as an Intune compliance-policy editor.

Cloud PC roles

Cloud PC Administrator and Cloud PC Reader can appear with Windows 365. They are additional workload roles, not two more of the nine standard Intune roles.

Role catalogs, feature availability and permission behavior can change. The original paper was researched in October 2026; verify the current Microsoft matrix, tenant licensing and effective permissions before assigning access.
