# Identity & Device Lifecycle

## Joiner

1. Create/verify identity in authoritative system.
2. Assign correct role/group/OU.
3. Apply licensing.
4. Enroll managed device.
5. Confirm baseline policy and MFA.
6. Validate required resources.
7. Record asset/user assignment.

## Mover

- change group/OU intentionally,
- remove obsolete access,
- validate new access,
- ensure inherited permissions do not create privilege accumulation.

## Leaver

- disable sign-in promptly,
- revoke sessions/tokens where authorized,
- recover organization-owned device,
- preserve/transfer data according to policy,
- remove privileged access,
- document completion.

## Device Retirement

- inventory confirmation,
- data handling,
- management unenrollment,
- wipe per organizational process,
- asset disposition record.
