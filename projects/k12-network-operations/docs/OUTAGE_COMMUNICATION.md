# Outage Communication Templates

## Initial

```text
We are investigating a connectivity issue affecting <scope> beginning at <time>. Local troubleshooting is in progress. The next update will include confirmed scope and the failing service layer.
```

## Technical Update

```text
Impact remains limited to <scope>. Local switching and gateway reachability are healthy. Testing indicates failure beyond <handoff/layer>. Evidence has been escalated to <team/vendor>. No customer action is required at this time.
```

## Resolution

```text
Service was restored at <time>. Root cause was <cause>. We validated <tests> from <sites/user groups>. We will <follow-up action> to reduce recurrence.
```
