# Reconstructing the System

`Reconstructing_the_System_First_Run_HUMINT_Expanded.docx` is the authoritative static first-run manuscript for a fictional nation-state HUMINT and intelligence-analysis project.

## Scope

- Approximately 100,500 words
- 1,000 canonical content blocks
- A 2018-2032 longitudinal intelligence narrative with 40 dated case events
- Twelve protected sources with distinct access, motivations, reliability histories, omissions, contradictions, and surviving contributions
- A 42-person covert network divided into eight directed operatives, fourteen knowing facilitators, and twenty participants with limited strategic knowledge
- Explicit treatment of circular reporting, source contamination, translation errors, missing context, negative evidence, analytic dissent, collection gaps, and legal boundaries
- A senior-analyst climax in which two real but unrelated events are separated before they can drive unnecessary military escalation
- A permanent-canon rule for later interactive Browser OS scenarios and alternate endings

## Portfolio truth boundary

The manuscript uses a fictional country, fictional people, synthetic reporting, and simulated institutions. It is a portfolio and training artifact, not operational intelligence, classified material, or a representation of government service.

## Integrity

The manuscript-generation workflow reported 28 passed integrity checks and zero accessibility findings. Full Word page-image rendering was unavailable in the managed generation environment, so exact pagination and final visual appearance depend on the reader's Word-compatible viewer.

## Canon and future simulation

This document remains the permanent canonical layer. Future interactive scenarios may reference stable characters, events, institutions, and identifiers while presenting alternate choices and endings. A scenario never overwrites the static manuscript.
