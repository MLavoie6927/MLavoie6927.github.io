Responsible technical use

Reference first. Verify before hardware.

  • Engineering: verify controlled requirements, current standards, exact material allowables, configuration, interfaces, uncertainty, and independent acceptance evidence.
  • Hazards: propulsion, pressure systems, cryogens, toxic materials, radiation, launch, and human-rated systems require authorized professional teams and facilities.
